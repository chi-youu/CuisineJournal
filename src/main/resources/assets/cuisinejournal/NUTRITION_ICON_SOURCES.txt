Nutrition icon sources
======================
The food icon is assembled from Minecraft 1.20.1's GUI hunger container
and half-food sprite. Minecraft assets remain the property of Mojang/Microsoft.

The saturation border is adapted from AppleSkin 2.5.1's full saturation HUD
overlay (textures/icons.png, rectangle 27,0,9,9), recolored to amber with a
new black outline and composed over the Minecraft half-food icon.
